JA HoopsConnect is the official app for the Jamaica Basketball Association (JBA). Stay connected with your league, track stats, and manage your team — all in one place.

FEATURES:

Board & Announcements
Stay up to date with the latest league news, announcements, and urgent communications from the JBA. Team reps can post updates and acknowledge important notices.

Live Stats & Box Scores
Follow game stats in real time. View detailed box scores, player performance breakdowns, and game-by-game results across all divisions.

Leaderboards & Player Cards
See who's leading the league in points, rebounds, assists, and more. Tap into detailed player cards with season averages and game logs.

Team Management
View rosters, team schedules, and division standings. Team reps can manage their squad and stay on top of league communications.

Calendar & Schedule
Never miss a game. View the full season calendar with dates, times, venues, and results for every matchup.

Push Notifications
Get notified about new announcements, upcoming games, stat approvals, and acknowledgment deadlines.

Built for the JBA community — players, coaches, team reps, statisticians, media, and fans.
