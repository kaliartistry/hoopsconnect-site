# Presentation team fixture sources

Snapshot date: 2026-09-14

The staging presentation contains 42 teams across three parent leagues. Every
score, standing, player name, and statistic remains synthetic. Reported team
names are reference data for demonstrating the product and are not presented as
confirmed 2026 registration lists.

## National Basketball League

The presentation uses the complete 10-team field reported when the NBL returned
in 2023:

- St George’s Slayers
- UWI Running Rebels
- Rae Town Raptors
- Urban Knights
- Upper Room Eagles
- Tivoli Wizards
- Falmouth Saints
- Central Celtics
- Spanish Town Spartans
- Portmore Flames

Primary source: [Jamaica Observer, 31 May 2023](https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2023/05/31/basketball-boss-elated-with-return-of-top-club-league-20231018-1729-151208/).
The [JBA 2024-2025 season page](https://www.basketballjamaica.org.jm/season/2024-2025/)
corroborates most of those club names, but it does not provide a clean current
registration roster.

## Women’s League

The [JBA competition overview](https://www.basketballjamaica.org.jm/our-league/)
confirms a JBA Women’s League, but no reliable current club roster was publicly
available during this review. The eight women’s clubs in staging are therefore
fictional presentation fixtures:

- Kingston Royals
- Portmore Queens
- Spanish Town Phoenix
- Montego Bay Tides
- Mandeville Monarchs
- St Andrew Suns
- Clarendon Comets
- St Ann Storm

They must be replaced with an association-approved roster before official use.

## School league

The 24 school names were drawn from recent ISSA basketball coverage, including
the [2025 urban finals](https://jamaica-gleaner.com/article/sports/20250302/georges-campion-lock-schoolboy-basketball-titles),
[2025 urban competition reporting](https://jamaica-star.com/article/sports/20251204/campion-notch-biggest-win-issa-basketball),
[2026 rural finals](https://jamaica-star.com/article/sports/20260209/rural-schools-take-shot-issa-basketball-finals),
and the [2026 schoolgirl final](https://jamaica-gleaner.com/article/sports/20260422/queens-lift-issa-schoolgirl-basketball-title).

The Division A, Division B, Division C, and Girls placement is synthetic. Its
purpose is to demonstrate that divisions sit beneath the School League parent;
it must not be described as the official ISSA divisional structure.
