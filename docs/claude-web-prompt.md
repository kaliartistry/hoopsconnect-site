# Prompt: Create Two Documents for JA HoopsConnect

Create two polished, professional PDF documents for **JA HoopsConnect** — a basketball league management platform built for the **Jamaica Basketball Association (JBA)** for the **NBL 2025-26 season**.

---

## DOCUMENT 1: ONE-PAGER

A single-page PDF overview. This is the JBA's first impression of the app. It must look like a real product sheet from a professional sports tech company — not a student project.

### Structure (top to bottom):

**Header:**
- App name: JA HoopsConnect
- Tagline suggestion: "The Digital Home of Jamaican Basketball" (or similar — make it punchy)
- JBA branding (use the color palette below)

**Vision Statement (2-3 sentences max):**
- Jamaica produces exceptional basketball talent, but there is zero digital infrastructure to make that talent visible. No accessible stats, no player profiles, no way for a college coach in Florida or a professional team in São Paulo to discover a player.
- HoopsConnect is the beginning of an ecosystem — not just an app, but the foundation for connecting Jamaican basketball to the world.

**Feature Sections (6 cards/blocks, paired in rows of 2):**
Each feature gets a clear title, a small icon/emoji, and 2-3 sentences emphasizing EASE and ACCURACY.

1. **The Board (Communication Hub)**
   - Central place for JBA announcements, schedule updates, venue changes
   - Acknowledgment tracking: critical notices require team reps to confirm they've seen them
   - Admins see exactly who has/hasn't acknowledged — eliminates "I didn't see it"
   - Automatic reminders sent before deadlines

2. **Stats & Box Scores**
   - Statisticians enter stats through the app during or after games (this is NOT fan-facing live streaming — it's designated stat entry by assigned people)
   - Post-game entry: select game → enter player stats in a grid → app validates totals → submit for admin approval → stats go live
   - Live game entry: courtside statistician taps actions in real time (2pt make, 3pt miss, rebound, assist, foul) → app tracks clock, substitutions, foul-outs → generates full box score automatically
   - Stats tracked: Points, Rebounds (off + def), Assists, Steals, Blocks, Fouls, Minutes
   - Stats are published after admin approval

3. **Leaderboards & Player Cards**
   - Automated league leaders: PPG, RPG, APG, SPG, BPG
   - Filter by division or league-wide
   - Player cards = digital resume: season averages, game-by-game log, team affiliation, trends
   - Shareable and verifiable — accessible to scouts, coaches, and teams worldwide
   - This is exactly what college recruiters and professional teams look for

4. **Standings & Schedules**
   - Live standings: W-L record, win %, games behind, streaks, last 10, playoff indicators
   - Season calendar (month or list view)
   - Auto-generated round-robin schedules (single/double/triple)
   - Manual game addition for playoffs and special events

5. **Media & Press Tools (PRIORITY — give this emphasis)**
   - Media needs a dedicated, reliable section with instant access to everything
   - They are a key distribution channel — the faster they get stats and summaries, the more coverage the league gets
   - Digital press credential card
   - Auto-generated game narrative summaries (final score, top performers, key stats)
   - Head-to-head comparisons (teams or players)
   - Stats export and copy-to-clipboard for quick publishing
   - Media should feel like they have a professional press box built into the app

6. **Team & League Management**
   - Create divisions (Premier, Women's, etc.)
   - Invite code system: generate codes tied to specific roles and teams
   - Usage limits (1, 5, 25 uses) and expiration dates
   - When someone signs up with a code → auto-assigned role + team
   - Push notifications: announcements, ack reminders, stat entry reminders, game day alerts

**The Bigger Picture (short section):**
- For the first time, a Jamaican basketball player's stats are accessible to anyone, anywhere
- US and Canadian college coaches can discover players through verifiable stats
- International professional teams get a reliable scouting resource
- A public website (planned) will serve as the single canonical source for all league stats — no downloading an app required, just click a link
- This opens doors that word-of-mouth and WhatsApp groups never could

**Cost Table (keep it compact):**

| Scale | Monthly Cost |
|-------|-------------|
| Up to 1,000 users | $0 |
| 5,000 users | $0 – $5 |
| 10,000 users | $2 – $15 |
| 50,000 users | $20 – $80 |

No servers to buy. No software licenses. No IT staff needed. Budget alerts notify before charges are incurred.

**Availability:**
- iPhone (TestFlight)
- Android (Google Play internal testing)
- Web (coming soon)

**Contributor Note (VERY BOTTOM — subtle, small, gray text, NOT bold):**
"HoopsConnect was built and donated to the JBA by Kali McCarthy — a former Jamaica national team player, NBL player, college player in the United States, and professional overseas. A contribution to the sport and to the potential of Jamaica's basketball."
- This must be understated. Small font. Not attention-grabbing. It's a quiet note about giving back, not a sales pitch.

### Design Requirements:
- Must fit on ONE page
- Professional sports-tech aesthetic — think official league product sheet
- Clean layout, strong typography, well-structured sections
- Use the brand colors below
- Emphasize EASE, ACCURACY, and ECOSYSTEM throughout the messaging

### Structure:

**Page 1: Cover**

- "Getting Started Guide" or "Onboarding Guide"

**Page 2: Getting Started**

**Page 3: The Board & Acknowledgments**
- How the communication hub works
- Types of posts (announcements, team updates, urgent notices)
- Acknowledgment tracking explained:
  - Admin posts a notice requiring acknowledgment
  - Team reps see the notice with a "Tap to Acknowledge" button
  - Admins see a tracker showing who has/hasn't acknowledged
  - Automatic reminders go out before deadlines
- Filters by division, type, etc.

**Page 4: Stats & Box Scores**
- Two workflows:
  1. **Post-game entry**: Statistician selects game → enters player stats in a grid → app validates totals (team totals must match) → submit for admin approval → stats go live
  2. **Live game entry**: Courtside statistician taps play-by-play actions (2pt make, 3pt miss, rebound, assist, steal, block, foul) → app tracks quarter/clock, substitutions, foul-outs → generates full box score automatically
- IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION: This is statistician-facing data entry, not a fan-facing live-score feature. Stats are entered by designated people and published after admin approval.
- Stats tracked: PTS, REB (off/def), AST, STL, BLK, FLS, MIN

**Page 5: Leaderboards, Player Cards & Standings**
- League leaders automatically computed: PPG, RPG, APG, SPG, BPG
- Filter by division or league-wide
- Player cards: season averages, game log, team info, trends
- Player cards are shareable digital resumes for scouts
- Standings: W-L, win %, GB, streaks, last 10, playoff indicators
- Calendar view (month/list), auto-generated round-robin schedules

**Page 6: Media & Press Tools + Team Management**
- MEDIA SECTION (give this prominence):
  - Dedicated section for media with instant access to everything
  - Digital press credential
  - Auto-generated game narrative summaries
  - Head-to-head comparisons
  - Stats export / copy to clipboard
  - Media are a distribution channel — this makes their job easy and the league more visible
- TEAM MANAGEMENT:
  - Creating divisions
  - Generating invite codes (role-specific, team-specific, usage limits, expiration)
  - Managing rosters
  - Push notification settings

**Page 7: Roles, Permissions & Cost**
- Quick-reference role table:

| Role | Access Method | What They Do |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| Admin | Invite code | Manage entire league — stats approval, schedules, users, divisions, invite codes |
| Team Rep | Invite code | Post team updates, acknowledge JBA notices, view team stats |
| Statistician | Invite code | Enter game statistics through the app, submit for approval |
| Media / Press | Invite code | Access press tools, generate summaries, export stats |
| Fan | Public signup | Follow standings, leaderboards, schedules, box scores |

- Cost summary (same table as one-pager)
- Contact info / getting help
- Contributor note (same as one-pager — bottom, subtle, small)

- Step-by-step workflow cards for stat entry and acknowledgment flows
