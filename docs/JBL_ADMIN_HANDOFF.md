# Jamaica Basketball League admin guide

## Prepare team registration

1. In **Admin → Leagues & Sponsors**, open **Jamaica Basketball League**. Use **View league hierarchy** to check that the correct division exists for the season.
2. In **Admin → Teams & Rosters**, choose **Create team**. Enter the official team name and select its league and division. Review the team before saving.
3. In **Admin → Invite Codes**, create a **Representative** invite for that team. Share the code with the assigned representative.

## Collect and review a roster

1. Representatives open **My Team → Team registration → Add player**. Enter the player’s name and jersey number, choose a single position if known, and add height, weight or kit sizes if available.
2. Use **Save player** after each entry. The draft stays on the team’s active-season page and is available when the representative signs in again.
3. Use **Review roster → Submit roster** to send the draft to league staff. Staff open the team through **Admin → Teams & Rosters**. Use **Mark reviewed** to confirm receipt or **Return to team** to request edits.

Keep dates of birth, contact information, signatures and supporting paperwork in the league’s existing registration process. Reviewing a submitted list records staff review in HoopsConnect.

## Update a league’s branding

1. Sign in with association management access. In **Admin → Leagues & Sponsors**, choose **Edit league**.
2. Change the league name or use the upload button next to **League logo** or **Sponsor logo**. PNG, JPG and WebP files up to 2 MB are accepted.
3. For a date-based switch, turn on **Schedule the next sponsor**, choose a start date, enter the new sponsor details, and select **Save league**. HoopsConnect shows the next sponsor from midnight in Jamaica. You can edit or cancel the upcoming change before its start date. You can schedule one upcoming sponsor per league.
