The Future of Jamaica Basketball
Jamaica Basketball Association

Jamaica has real basketball talent. The problem is that no one outside the island can see it. There are no accessible stats, no digital footprint, no way for a college scout or international team to follow a player's development. HoopsConnect changes that — giving every player, every game, and every season a permanent, shareable record visible to the world.

What It Does

📊

Live Stats & Box Scores

Record game stats in real time or after. Full box scores with PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, MIN for every player.

🏆

Leaderboards & Player Cards

Automated league leaders across 5 categories. Player cards with season averages and game logs.

📋

Standings & Schedules

Live standings with W-L, streaks, playoff indicators. Auto-generated round-robin schedules.

📣

Board & Acknowledgments

Central communication hub with read-receipt tracking. Know who's seen every critical notice.

👥

Team & League Management

Divisions, rosters, invite codes, user roles. One admin panel to run the entire league.

🔔

Push Notifications

Automatic alerts for announcements, game reminders, stat deadlines, and ack requests.

🌎 The Bigger Picture — Why Stats Open Doors

🎓

College Scouts

US & Canadian colleges can discover and recruit talent by following stats all season long.

🌎

International Teams

Professional teams from Brazil, Argentina, Mexico can identify and track Jamaican prospects.

⭐

Player Exposure

Every player gets a digital resume. 22 PPG in the NBL Premier deserves to be seen by the world.
