JA HoopsConnect

Feature Guide & Board Presentation
Jamaica Basketball Association

HoopsConnect is a mobile and web application purpose-built for the Jamaica Basketball Association. It replaces spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and word-of-mouth with a single platform where administrators, team representatives, statisticians, media, and fans all have the tools they need to run and follow the league.

RoleWhat They Can Do
AdminManage the entire league — stats, schedules, users, divisions
Team RepPost team updates, acknowledge notices, view team stats
StatisticianEnter and manage game statistics
Media / PressAccess press tools, generate summaries, export stats
FanFollow standings, leaderboards, schedules, and box scores

Getting Started

Setup in 30 Seconds
  • Download the app — iPhone users via TestFlight, Android users via Google Play
  • Create your account — Sign up with email, Google, or Apple ID
  • Use an invite code — Team reps, statisticians, and media receive a 6-character code from the JBA that automatically assigns their role and team
  • No code needed for fans — Just sign up and start following the league

Standings & Schedules

Always Up to Date
  • Live standings — W-L record, win percentage, games behind, streak, last 10 games, points for/against
  • Playoff indicators — Top 4 teams highlighted as postseason contenders
  • Full calendar — Month view or list view. Tap past games for box scores, see upcoming fixtures with times and venues
  • Auto schedule generation — Admins generate single/double/triple round-robin schedules. Pick game days, time slots, and venue — the app handles the rest

Team & League Management

Run the League from Your Phone
  • Division management — Create Premier, Women's, or any custom division
  • Invite codes — Generate codes for team reps. Set usage limits and expiration. When someone signs up with a code, they're automatically assigned to the right team with the right permissions
  • User roles — Admin, Rep, Statistician, Media, Press, Fan — each sees only what they need
  • Season management — Create new seasons, archive completed ones, switch active seasons

🌎 The Vision — Why This Matters

The Problem

Jamaica produces exceptional basketball talent. Every year, players compete in the NBL and high school leagues with skill and athleticism that rival anywhere in the Caribbean. But almost none of them are seen beyond Jamaica's borders. There are no accessible stats, no player profiles, no way for a college coach in Florida or a professional team in São Paulo to look up a player's averages.

The Person Behind It

Kali McCarthy understands this problem intimately. As a former Jamaica national team player, NBL competitor, junior national team member, high school player, college basketball player in the United States, and professional overseas — Kali has lived every stage of the basketball journey from Kingston to the world.

Jamaica National Team NBL Player Junior National Team High School Player US College Basketball Professional Overseas

That experience revealed a clear truth: the gap is not in the talent — it's in the systems. Countries with proper stats tracking produce more recruited players, not because they have better talent, but because their talent is visible.

The Opportunity

🎓

College Scouts

US & Canadian colleges can follow players all season — not just at a single showcase

🌎

International Pro Teams

Teams from Brazil, Argentina, Mexico can identify and track Jamaican prospects

⭐

Player Exposure

Every player gets a digital resume — proof of performance, shareable worldwide
