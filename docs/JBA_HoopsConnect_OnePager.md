# JA HoopsConnect

### The Future of Jamaica Basketball

---

## The Vision

Jamaica has real basketball talent. The problem is that no one outside the island can see it. There are no accessible stats, no digital footprint, no way for a college scout in the US or a professional team in Brazil to follow a player's development. **HoopsConnect changes that.** It gives every player, every game, and every season a permanent, shareable record — visible to the world.

---

## What It Does

- **Live Stats & Box Scores** — Record game stats in real time or after the game. Full box scores with points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, and minutes for every player.

- **Leaderboards & Player Cards** — Automated league leaders across 5 categories. Individual player cards with season averages and game-by-game logs — exactly what a scout needs.

- **Standings & Schedules** — Live league standings with win-loss records, streaks, and playoff indicators. Full season calendar with auto-generated round-robin schedules.

- **Board & Announcements** — A central communication hub for the league. Post announcements, track who has acknowledged critical notices, and keep every team rep in the loop.

- **Team & League Management** — Create divisions, manage rosters, generate schedules, and onboard team reps with invite codes. One admin panel to run the entire league.

- **Push Notifications** — Automatic alerts for new announcements, game reminders, stat deadlines, and acknowledgment requests.

---

## The Bigger Picture

When Jamaica's basketball stats are digitized and accessible, doors open:

- **US & Canadian colleges** can discover and recruit Jamaican talent by following their stats throughout a season — not just from a single showcase.

- **International professional teams** from Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, and across the Americas can identify and track players they're interested in.

- **Players get exposure** they simply don't have today. A 19-year-old averaging 22 points per game in the NBL Premier Division deserves to be seen.

Proper systems create proper opportunities. HoopsConnect is the system.

## What It Costs

HoopsConnect runs on Firebase, Google's cloud platform. There are no servers to buy, no IT staff to hire, and no software licenses to pay for.

| Scale | Monthly Cost |
|-------|-------------|
| Current (testing) | **$0** |
| 1,000 users | **$0** |
| 5,000 users | **$0 - $5** |
| 10,000 users | **$2 - $15** |

The only ongoing cost is the cloud database — and at the scale of Jamaican basketball, it falls well within the free tier.

## A Contribution to Jamaica Basketball

HoopsConnect was built and donated by **Kali McCarthy** — a former Jamaica national team player, NBL player, junior national team player, high school player, college basketball player in the United States, and professional overseas.

Having lived the journey from high school courts in Jamaica to professional basketball abroad, Kali has an intimate understanding of where the talent gap lies — and it's not in the talent itself. It's in the systems. Jamaican players are as gifted as anywhere in the Caribbean, but without proper tracking, stats, and visibility, they remain undiscovered.

Kali believes that having proper systems like HoopsConnect will allow not only colleges to tap in and see and follow players, but also international teams from places like Brazil, Argentina, and Mexico to follow players they are interested in and track their stats.

This app is a contribution to the sport Kali loves and believes in — and to the potential of Jamaica's basketball.
