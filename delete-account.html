To request deletion of your HoopsConnect account and all associated data, send an email to the address below.

What to include

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Subject line: "Delete My Account"

What gets deleted

  • Your user profile and authentication credentials
  • Board posts and acknowledgment records
  • Any associated team data

Processing time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been removed.
