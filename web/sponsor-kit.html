Main sponsor

Foska Oats

Foska Oats is a Jamaican brand from Caribbean Foods Limited. Its range includes rolled oats and instant oatmeal. Foska Oats supports the Jamaica Basketball League as its main sponsor.

Visit the official Foska brand page

App donor

Kali McCarthy

Kali represented Jamaica on the national basketball team. He played for St. George’s College and for St. George’s Slayers in the National Basketball League, now the Jamaica Basketball League.

He built and donated HoopsConnect to support the people who run Jamaican basketball and the fans who follow it. His other ventures include ShipSafe SDK, EventSync Day-Of, and Kali Artistry, which includes Kali Love Stories.
